About ChennAIR Pollution Forecasting
Chennai faces significant air pollution challenges due to rapid industrialization, vehicular traffic, and coastal weather patterns. Our AI-powered system fuses satellite data, meteorological inputs, and deep learning models to provide accurate 72-hour AQI forecasts — helping citizens, health agencies, and policymakers make informed decisions.
